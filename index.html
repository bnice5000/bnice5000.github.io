    Welcome to NineTenLevins!#

    This is where I stash the small things that refuse to stop tapping on my shoulder. Field notes, playlists, essays, stray curiosities, and the week’s wandering thoughts all settle in beside the predictable misadventures. If you enjoy mild introspection paired with socially awkward commentary, you will feel right at home, or at least amused at your own choices.
